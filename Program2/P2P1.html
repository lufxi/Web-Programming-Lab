About ILAHIA

ILAHIA College of Engineering and Technology is a private institute, established in 2002 in Kerala - Other, Kerala. ILAHIA College of Engineering and Technology is approved by AICTE and offers a total of 24 courses at UG and PG levels in 14 specialisations such as Computer Science Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Information Technology, Civil Engineering and Electrical Engineering. Furthermore, it offer courses such as B.E. / B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MBA/PGDM and MCA in Full Time mode. ILAHIA College of Engineering and Technology fee structure includes a total tuition fee ranging from INR 1,00,000 to 2,00,000. ILAHIA College of Engineering and Technology has an annual seat intake of 867 for all the programmes. It offers courses, in streams such as Engineering, Business and Management Studies and IT and Software. The institute has a safe and well-equipped campus with all the advanced facilities.
